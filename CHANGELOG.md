## v0.1.0 - 2026-11-24
### Added
- Daily screen time limits per user, set for each day of the week. Only playing video counts, not pauses or music
- When time runs out, playback stops right away or the current video finishes, optionally up to a grace limit. New playback is refused until the next day
- Messages on the playing device: a warning before time runs out, a heads-up when the current video is the last one, and the time left when playback starts
- Extra time for today (+10, +15, +30 or no limit) and a status overview of every limited user on the settings page
- Activity log entries when time runs out and when extra time is added or removed
